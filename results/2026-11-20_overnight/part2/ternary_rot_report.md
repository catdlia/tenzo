# `ternary_rot` KV mode in the engine (block 4)

Implementation (`FusedKVCache`, `src/runtime/Hadamard.h`, `HadamardSigns.h`): K (after RoPE) and V are rotated by R = H128 diag(signs)/sqrt(128)
(AVX2 FWHT; the signs are the torch seed-0 signs of the simulator, generated by `tools/reference/gen_hadamard_signs.py`), quantized
to ternary per group of 32 (threshold 0.7 mean|y|, scale = mean |y| on the support, fp32 scale per group, 2-bit codes), q is rotated
with the same R, and the attention output of the ternary part is un-rotated once per head. The first 4 tokens (sinks) and the 32 most
recent tokens are served from a per-token INT8 copy (4 + 32 slots per layer and KV head; a token's ternary code is computed at append time
from the exact fp32 k/v, because quantizing it later from the INT8 copy would change the numbers; decision log D11). Optional calibrated
clip multipliers load from `TENZO_KV_ALPHA=<file>` (`tools/reference/export_kv_alpha.py`); not used in the measurements below.
Existing KV modes are untouched. `--kv-quant ternary_rot`, accepted by `tenzo_create_engine` (needs head_dim 128).

Gate "engine PPL equals the simulator replica (paired ratio, CI contains 1)":
- **UK8: passed** (raw ratio 1.0053 [0.9966, 1.0144]).
- **EN16: the literal gate fails by a hair** (raw ratio 1.0041 [1.0007, 1.0077]; the CI excludes 1 by 0.0007). The cause is the engine's own baseline,
  not the KV scheme: the engine with the INT8 embedding/LM head is +0.4% above golden fp32 even in `int8_fused` (19.141 vs 19.066). Dividing it
  out (engine ternary_rot / engine int8_fused against simulator S3+s4r32 / golden fp32, same paired bootstrap): ratio **1.0002 [0.9957, 1.0046] (EN16),
  1.0033 [0.9930, 1.0124] (UK8)**, both contain 1 (`ppl_ternary_rot/compare_en16.txt`, `ppl_ternary_rot_uk/compare_uk8.txt`, `scripts/compare_engine_sim.py`).
  Reported as it is: the specified gate is met on UK, and on EN only after removing the known baseline offset.
