## Observations & Caveats

- **Engine vs Golden parity holds at ctx = 2048**: `int8_fused` (`×0.9998` EN, `×0.9993` UK vs `golden_fp32`) and `ternary_rot` (baseline-adjusted engine/sim ratio `1.0006 [0.9965, 1.0034]` EN, `1.0030 [0.9991, 1.0083]` UK) match the Python golden simulator within statistical noise across full 2048-token contexts.
- **Asymmetric positional drift**: on EN, `engine ternary_rot` vs `int8_fused` stays bounded across position blocks (`×1.0673` -> `×1.0557` -> `×1.0860` -> `×1.0659`), and `S13+s4r32` stays near `×1.000–1.018`. On UK, `engine ternary_rot` degrades monotonically with context position (`×1.1443` in `1..512`, `×1.1650` in `513..1024`, `×1.2432` in `1025..1536`, `×1.3051` in `1537..2047`; `golden S3+s4r32` `×1.1382` -> `×1.3019`), and `S13+s4r32` rises from `×1.0866` (`1..512`) to `×1.1358` (`1537..2047`).
- **Calibrated mix alphas (`alpha_mix.txt`) generalize to ctx = 2048 without overfitting**: calibrated on 512-token EN+UK windows, `ternary_rot+alpha_mix` lowers full PPL ratio from `×1.0687` to `×1.0381` (EN) and from `×1.2127` to `×1.1643` (UK), improving every block (UK `1537..2047` drops from `×1.3051` to `×1.2511`), while not removing the monotonic UK position growth.
- **Memory budget**: Tenzo engine `PROC_HWM_KB` stays below 1 GB at `ctx=2048` (`950.4–950.7 MB` `int8_fused`; `902.3–902.8 MB` `ternary_rot`, ~48 MB smaller than `int8_fused`).
- **Caveat**: each language has `N = 4` long windows (8,188 scored tokens), so 4-window bootstrap CIs are coarser than at ctx=512 (`N = 16` / `32`), though the directional block-by-block EN-vs-UK divergence is consistent across both golden and engine runs.

## Reproducibility & Environment

- Windows: `python3 scripts/make_windows_2048.py` -> `results/reference/windows_en_2048.ids` (`sha256: 66bc4ca09d6e49d3...`), `results/reference/windows_uk_2048.ids` (`sha256: 403a0f9a71daa621...`).
- Golden: `python3 scripts/run_h3_ctx2048.py --mode golden-all --batch 4 --threads 4` (256-row chunked log-softmax, peak RSS 3.01–3.55 GB under `MemoryMax=4G`).
- Engine: `python3 scripts/run_h3_ctx2048.py --mode engine-all` (`-c 2048 --prefill batched`, with `TENZO_KV_ALPHA=/app/results/2026-11-23_agent2/H1/alpha_mix.txt` for `ternary_rot+alpha_mix`).
- Analysis: `python3 scripts/analyze_h3_ctx2048.py` (CPU: `12th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i3-1215U`, governor: `powersave`, binary built on `agent2/2026-11-23`).
